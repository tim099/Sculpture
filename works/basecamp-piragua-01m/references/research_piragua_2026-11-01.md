## 讀數

### 尺寸
- 〔核〕D 第 8 章（1685，Quibo 附近的島上自己造）："Captain Davis made two very large canoes; one was 36 foot long and five or six feet wide; the other 32 foot long and near as wide as the other."
- 〔核〕D 第 8 章，同一段，殼厚："we left the bottoms commonly about three inches thick, and the sides two inches thick below and one and a half at the top. One or both of the ends we sharpen to a point."
- 〔轉述〕R：達連一艘小獨木舟約 20 呎、寬不到 1.5 呎，載 5 人（"an egg-shell" 〔核〕）。
- 〔轉述、可疑〕Wikipedia "Pirogue" 引 Little 2014 p.169："30 feet long, 16-18 feet wide" —— 寬度不合理，不用。
- 吃水、乾舷：沒找到一手數字。

### 船型與造法
- 〔核〕D 第 3 章："We reckon the periagos and canoes that are made of cedar to be the best of any; they are nothing but the tree itself made hollow boat-wise, with a flat bottom, and the canoe generally sharp at both ends, the periago at one only, with the other end flat."
  ⇒ **periago ＝ 平底的獨木舟，一頭尖、一頭平**（跟兩頭尖的 canoe 分開的就是這一點）。
- 〔轉述〕D 第 8 章：先把大樹「squaring the uppermost side」再挖 ⇒ 舷頂是平的一條。
- 〔核〕E 第 VII 章："cedars, seven or eight feet about, of which they can build boats and ships, so as to bear only one great sail; such vessels being called piraguas."
- 〔轉述〕剖開加寬、插底板、加高舷板的做法是 18 世紀卡羅萊納的 periagua，沒找到 17 世紀加勒比的出處 ⇒ 不用。

### 動力
- 〔核〕D 第 1 章："a periago (or large canoe) that rowed with 14 oars"。
- 〔核〕D 第 5 章："These canoes were fitted with thwarts or benches, straps and oars fit for service; ... which we fixed for landing men when occasion required." ⇒ 槳靠繩環（straps）掛著，沒提槳栓。
- 〔核〕R 第 VII 章（Perico 1680-04-23）："Our two periaguas being heavy could not row so fast as we that were in the canoes"。
- 〔核〕R 第 V 章：印第安人的大獨木舟 "had also fitted a very good sail"。
- 帆型：一手出處都沒寫；H 畫的是 1744 年的西班牙大型 piragua（兩桅、像拉丁帆、每舷 12–14 槳），不是海盜的獨木舟。

### 載人
- 〔核〕D 第 8 章（Realejo 1685）："520 of us went away in 31 canoes"（平均 17 人）。
- 〔核〕R 第 VII 章："our lesser periaguas, in which were thirty-two or more of our company"。
- 〔核〕R 第 V 章：那艘大獨木舟 "better able to carry twenty men"。
- 〔核〕E 第 XVII 章（1670-08-18 摩根從查格雷斯出發去巴拿馬）："He had with him twelve hundred men, five boats laden with artillery, and thirty-two canoes."

### 武器
- R 第 VII 章 Perico 那一仗：獨木舟上用火槍。
- 〔核〕E 第 V 章：Bartholomew Portugues "a boat of thirty men and four small guns" —— 是 boat，不是獨木舟。
- 迴旋砲：只有 Little（經 Wikipedia）說 "one cannon and four swivel guns"，沒核 ⇒ 船頭一門迴旋砲是**設計決定**。

### 海盜怎麼用
- 〔核〕Perico 1680（R 第 VII 章）：五艘獨木舟加兩艘 periagua、共 68 人，迎戰西班牙的小軍艦並登船（"laid them on board" 那句 agent 引了，basecamp 沒找到原句）。
- 〔核〕Santa Maria 1680（R）："We all embarked in thirty-five canoes, and a periagua, which we had taken here lying at anchor before the town."
- 〔核〕Realejo 1685（D 第 8 章）：31 艘獨木舟、520 人從大船划進港。
- 〔轉述〕摩根 1670–71 年沿查格雷斯河往上：河水太淺，船留下來，人改搭獨木舟（E 第 XVII 章）。
