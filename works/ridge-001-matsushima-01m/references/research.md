Scope: numbers and sources for a 0.1 m/voxel model of the ship as she was at the Yalu (17 Sept 1894).
Research time: about 25 minutes, 2026-10-21.

### Photo-measurement method (P1895)

- The photo is close to a true broadside. Visible hull from stern tip (x ≈ 20 px) to stem head (x ≈ 1867 px) is 1847 px.
- I took that as about 98 m (LOA 99.2 m minus a small ram projection), giving **about 18.85 px/m**.
- The waterline sits at y ≈ 898 px.
- All "photo est." heights below come from this scale. Treat them as **±10–15 %**: the scale is assumed, the draught on the day is unknown, and there is some perspective.

### 12 cm (4.7 in) QF battery

- **Model:** Armstrong 1887 type, 12 cm/40.
- **Total on Matsushima: 12 guns, 6 per side.** Her sisters had 11.

| Position | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Middle/gun deck | **5 per side** in casemate ports in the hull side, all in the **forward half** of the ship, ahead of the mast. The magazine and barbette take the after part | JA-class (「中甲板上に砲郭配置で5基」); EN-ship ("ten on the gun deck, five to each side"); Jane06 plan and profile; P1896 shows 5 big ports, forward half |
| Bow (chase guns) | **1 per side** in upper-deck embrasures, firing ahead | JA-class (「艦首上部に1基」); EN-ship ("upper deck embrasures on either side of the bow"); Jane06 plan shows two 4.7s angled forward at the bow |
| Correction | EN-class claims Matsushima's 12th gun was "on the fantail". This **contradicts** Jane06, JA-class and EN-ship. Disregard it: Matsushima had no 12 cm gun aft | n/a |

- **Port shape:** Matsushima and Hashidate had **narrow, tall ports**, designed for slow-firing rifled guns. Itsukushima's ports were wide (horizontal). Source: JA-class.
- **Gun shields:** 110 mm. Training 150°, elevation +20° / −3°. Source: JA-class, NP.
- **Ammunition:** 120 rounds per gun. Source: EN.
- **Fire arcs:** ahead 2 × 12 cm + 1 × 47 mm; broadside 1 × 32 cm + 6 × 12 cm; astern 1 × 32 cm only. Source: JA-class.

### Light guns (sources conflict)

| Source | Matsushima light guns |
|---|---|
| EN-ship, text (Conway's) | **6 × 6-pdr (57 mm) Hotchkiss in sponsons on the upper deck** + **11 × 3-pdr (47 mm) "at various locations"** |
| EN-ship, infobox | 6 × 3-pdr + 2 × 5-barrel 1-pdr Hotchkiss revolvers |
| EN-class | 5–6 × 6-pdr in upper-deck sponsons + 2–5 × 3-pdr |
| NP (paraphrased) | 6 × 57 mm/40 6-pdr + **11 × 37 mm 1-pdr Hotchkiss** |
| JA-ship, infobox (no footnote) | 6 × 47 mm + **2 × 37 mm 5-barrel revolvers** |
| JA-class | 6 × "7.6 cm QF" (3 per side; probably a later or mis-named fit) + 6 × 47 mm Hotchkiss |
| Brassey02 (sisters) | 3 × 6-pdr per side in small sponsons on the upper deck amidships, above the main battery |

- **Positions:** fighting tops carried 37–47 mm anti-torpedo-boat guns (JA-class says this generically for military masts, not specifically for Matsushima). Bridge and upper-deck positions are visible but not countable in the photos.
- **Best reading:** 6 × 57 mm in upper-deck/forecastle sponsons (3 per side), plus a mix of 47 mm singles and 37 mm revolvers in the tops, on the bridge and in the boats. **Exact positions are unknown.**

### Torpedo tubes

- **Count and type:** 4 × 356 mm (14 in), above water, fixed. Source: JA-ship, JA-class, EN, NP.
- **Arrangement (conflict):**
  - **Best:** **1 bow, 1 stern, 2 broadside (1 per side)**. Source: JA-class; NP; Jane06 profile (T at the bow, T at the stern, ○T broadside near the barbette, about 74 m from the stem).
  - **Alternative:** 3 bow + 1 stern. Source: EN-ship and EN-class (Conway's).
- **Torpedoes carried:** 20. Source: EN.
- **At the Yalu:** a dud 26 cm shell from *Pingyuan*, on the starboard side, wrecked the torpedo tubes there. Source: EN-ship (Perry 1964).

## 10. Battle of the Yalu (17 Sept 1894): points relevant to the model

- **Role:** flagship of the Combined Fleet, flying the flag of **Vice-Admiral Itō Sukeyuki**. Captain was 尾本知道 (Omoto Tomomichi). She opened fire at 12:52 and fired her 32 cm gun **only 4 times**. Sources: JA 黄海海戦 article; EN-ship (Perry 1964).
- **Main hit:** at **about 15:30** a 30.5 cm shell from ***Zhenyuan*** struck the **port No. 4 12 cm casemate** on the gun (middle) deck, which is in the **forward battery**. It set off the stacked charges, killing 28 men and wounding 68 at that gun group (EN); 3 other guns were put out of action. Total ship losses were **57 killed and 54 wounded** (EN, Paine; JA-ship gives 57 killed). Sources: JA-ship; JA 黄海海戦; EN-ship. The Kaiyodai photo-collection chronology says 「前部の船体上部が破壊」 ("the upper hull forward was wrecked").
- **Other damage:** a dud 26 cm shell from *Pingyuan* on the starboard side wrecked the torpedo tubes and killed 4. A second 30.5 cm shell passed through without exploding. Small-calibre hits damaged the **funnel, masts and deck fittings**. Source: EN-ship (Perry 1964).
- **Aftermath:** Itō hoisted the "not under command" flag (不関旗) and shifted his flag to ***Hashidate***. Matsushima was repaired at Kure and returned to service after 26 days. Source: EN-ship; JA 黄海海戦.
